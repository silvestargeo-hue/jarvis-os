Quick start

60-second tour

1 · Boot & unlock

Hit Launch OS. Watch the boot sequence. First run sets no PIN — click System → Set PIN (or pin 1234 in Terminal) and it will be asked on next boot.

2 · Talk to JARVIS

Open AI Chat from the dock. Stream answers with no key. Try /image a neon city in the rain — the painting lands in the bubble, your Files, and Image Lab.

3 · See & speak

📎 attaches an image for vision. 🎙 starts voice input — your sentence is transcribed and sent. 🔊 reads the last answer aloud.

4 · Get real work done

Notes persist forever. Tasks get AI-broken into subtasks. Files stores paintings and mic memos. Everything survives reloads — try it.

Privacy: your notes, tasks, chat history, files and PIN never leave your device — they live in your browser's localStorage. AI requests go directly from your browser to the model endpoints; there is no JARVIS server in the middle.
